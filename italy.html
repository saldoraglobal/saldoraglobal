Know before you book

What trips up shipments from Italy.

The issues most likely to add cost or delay on this lane.

Wine and spirits rules

Alcohol importers need a federal importer's basic permit and TTB label approval before the first shipment arrives.

FDA for foods

Olive oil, cheese and other foods need FDA facility registration and prior notice.

Section 232 duties

Steel, aluminum and some auto parts can carry Section 232 duties.

Paperwork

Documents to have ready

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading, issued by the licensed forwarder / NVOCC or carrier
  • ISF data to your filer 24 hours before loading
  • FDA prior notice for food and drink; TTB label approval and importer permit for wine

Saldora Global coordinates documents through our partner; it does not file the ISF or customs entry.

Duty rates

Your rate depends on the HTS code, and in 2026 it has kept changing.

In February 2026 the Supreme Court ruled that tariffs imposed under the emergency-powers law (IEEPA) were not authorized, and a temporary replacement surcharge took effect on 24 February. Don't rely on a rate you saw months ago.
