Express courier––Volume ÷ 5,000, the usual courier divisor.
Ocean LCL––Weight or measure: 1 CBM is charged as 1,000 kg, whichever is higher.
Step 2

Container capacity estimate

This compares your total volume and weight with each container's internal volume and typical payload. It does not check whether individual cartons fit through the doors or stack safely; the partner forwarder confirms the load plan. The marker at 85% is a practical loading limit. Payload limits vary by carrier and road-weight rules.
