Know before you book

What trips up shipments from Japan.

The issues most likely to add cost or delay on this lane.

Section 232 duties

Vehicles, auto parts, steel and aluminum can carry Section 232 duties.

Food rules

Japanese foods and beverages need FDA registration and prior notice, and some ingredients need extra permits.

Classifying components

Electronic parts and assemblies are often misclassified, which changes the duty and draws audits later.

What the US buys

Main imports from Japan

  • Vehicles and auto parts
  • Industrial machinery
  • Electronics and components
  • Specialty foods and beverages

Paperwork

Documents to have ready

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading, issued by the licensed forwarder / NVOCC or carrier
  • ISF data to your filer 24 hours before loading
  • FDA prior notice for food and drink

Saldora Global coordinates documents through our partner; it does not file the ISF or customs entry.
