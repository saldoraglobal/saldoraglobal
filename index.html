What We Do

Freight sales, done properly.

Saldora Global is a sales agency, not a carrier. We find the right sailing and price for your cargo through FMC-licensed partners, help you understand the tariff picture before you commit — and stay with the shipment until it is delivered.

Free Tariff Consultation — by Email Free

Before you place an order, email us the basics of your shipment. We review the current US tariff landscape for your origin country, how duties are calculated, what Section 301 and reciprocal tariffs mean in practice, and the classification questions your goods raise — and reply in writing, so you know the shape of the bill before the cargo sails. It is general information only: final classification and duty are always confirmed by a licensed customs broker.

Free · by email · written reply · no obligation

FCL — Full Container Load

Dedicated 20ft and 40ft containers for importers with regular or larger-volume cargo. We match your shipment to sailings and equipment through our partner forwarder, and confirm the full landed picture before you commit.

Best for regular importers

LCL — Less Than Container Load

Smaller shipment? Share container space and pay for the volume you use. A practical way for growing importers to bring in samples, first orders, and mixed cargo without waiting to fill a container.

Best for smaller & first shipments

Door-to-Door Coordination

Port-to-port is rarely the whole journey. Through our partner forwarder we coordinate the full movement — origin handling, ocean leg, US arrival, and final delivery to your warehouse or door — as one managed booking.

One booking, start to finish

Customs-Clearance Coordination

US customs entry is handled by licensed customs brokers. We coordinate your documents — commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading — with the broker through our partner, so clearance is ready when the vessel arrives, not after.

Via licensed customs brokers

Free tariff consultation — by email.

US tariffs on imported goods have changed repeatedly and can shift with little notice. Email us the basics of your shipment and our freight desk will review the tariff picture for your lane and your goods — and reply in writing, free of charge, before you book anything.

What to send us

  • What the goods are — a plain description is fine, plus how they are packed if you know it
  • Origin port & country — where the cargo ships from (e.g. Nhava Sheva, India)
  • US destination — port, city, or delivery ZIP
  • FCL or LCL, and rough volume / weight — container size, or total kg and CBM; an estimate is fine
  • Any timing pressure — a ready date, a hard deadline, or simply "no rush"

What you receive

  • A written reply reviewing what the tariff picture looks like for your lane and your goods — the duties and additional tariffs that may apply, dated to the day we write
  • The classification and duty questions your shipment raises — what will need pinning down, and why it matters to your landed cost
  • What to verify before booking — with your supplier and with a licensed customs broker — so nothing surprises you at entry

What it is — and is not

  • General information only. The consultation is not a binding HTS classification and not a final duty figure — both are confirmed by a licensed customs broker for your actual entry.
  • No freight rates or bookings. The consultation itself produces no rates and books nothing. If you want pricing afterwards, it comes separately, in writing, from our FMC-licensed partner.
  • Not customs brokerage. We do not file entries with US Customs and Border Protection or act for you before CBP.
  • Not legal advice. For legal questions about trade remedies or compliance, consult a trade attorney.
  • No guarantees. Tariff rates can change after we reply. Every figure we share is dated and marked as an estimate.

Consultations are general educational guidance to help you plan. We work alongside licensed customs brokers and introduce one if you need one.

How It Works

From enquiry to delivery, in four steps.

No portals to learn, no call centres. You deal with Saldora; our FMC-licensed partner forwarder / NVOCC operates the booking.

01

Enquiry & free tariff review

Tell us the origin, destination, cargo, and ready date — two minutes, no obligation. Ask for the free email tariff consultation and we will review the duty picture in writing before you decide.

02

Quote from a licensed partner

Our partner forwarder / NVOCC prices the shipment. You receive a clear quotation with its validity and what it includes — no rate is ever quoted before the partner confirms it.

03

Booking & documents

On your approval, the partner books the sailing and issues the transport documents. We coordinate paperwork and keep you updated as the cargo moves.

04

Arrival & delivery

Customs entry is coordinated through licensed brokers, and delivery is arranged to your door or warehouse. One contact — us — the whole way.
