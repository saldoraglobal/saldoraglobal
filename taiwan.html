Know before you book

What trips up shipments from Taiwan.

The issues most likely to add cost or delay on this lane.

Anti-dumping on hardware

Steel threaded rod, steel nails and some other steel hardware from Taiwan carry anti-dumping duties.

Origin of electronics

Boards built in Taiwan from Chinese components can raise origin questions. Keep records of where the main processing happens.

Export controls

Some advanced chips and equipment carry US export-control obligations.

Documents to have ready
