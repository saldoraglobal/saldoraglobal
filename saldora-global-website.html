Live Tariff Consultation

Know the tariff picture before the cargo sails.

US tariffs on imported goods have changed repeatedly and can shift with little notice. A short live call can save an importer from a landed cost they never planned for. That conversation is what we lead with.

What a consultation covers

  • The current US tariff landscape for your origin country — including Section 301, reciprocal and other additional duties, dated to the day we speak
  • How US duty is worked out: customs value, duty rate, additional tariffs, and fees such as the Merchandise Processing and Harbor Maintenance fees
  • A landed-cost scenario built from your figures — freight, insurance, duty and fees — so you can compare origins or suppliers
  • Incoterms and who pays what, from the supplier's door to yours
  • The questions to take to your licensed customs broker, so nothing surprises you at entry
Book a Live Consultation Request a Quote

What it is not

  • Not customs brokerage. We do not file entries with US Customs and Border Protection or act for you before CBP.
  • No binding classification. We explain how classification works, but the final HTS code and duty rate for your entry are confirmed by a licensed customs broker.
  • Not legal advice. For legal questions about trade remedies or compliance, consult a trade attorney.
  • No guarantees. Tariff rates can change after we speak. Every figure we share is dated and marked as an estimate.

Consultations are general educational guidance to help you plan. We work alongside licensed customs brokers and introduce one if you need one.

How It Works

From enquiry to delivery, in four steps.

No portals to learn, no call centres. You deal with Saldora; our FMC-licensed partner forwarder / NVOCC operates the booking.

01

Enquiry & tariff call

Tell us the origin, destination, cargo, and ready date — two minutes, no obligation. Ask for a live tariff consultation and we will talk through duties before you decide.

02

Quote from a licensed partner

Our partner forwarder / NVOCC prices the shipment. You receive a clear quotation with its validity and what it includes — no rate is ever quoted before the partner confirms it.

03

Booking & documents

On your approval, the partner books the sailing and issues the transport documents. We coordinate paperwork and keep you updated as the cargo moves.

04

Arrival & delivery

Customs entry is coordinated through licensed brokers, and delivery is arranged to your door or warehouse. One contact — us — the whole way.

Our founder has — he brings years of experience in global shipping to every enquiry, including as General Manager of a container-shipping business in Mumbai (2009–2011), where he saw from the inside how freight is priced and booked — and exactly where importer money leaks away.
